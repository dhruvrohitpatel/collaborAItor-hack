# Collabor-AI-tor 2-Minute Demo Script

## 0:00 - 0:20: Problem + Positioning

"Project-based classes need strong team formation, but instructors often have limited visibility into student collaboration styles. Collabor-AI-tor augments instructor judgment with transparent AI-assisted profiling and team support tools."

## 0:20 - 0:55: Instructor Flow

1. Open `/instructor`
2. Click **Load Demo Seed**
3. Point out student roster + dashboard metrics
4. Click **Generate Profiles**
5. Mention that AI outputs are descriptive and reviewable, not prescriptive

## 0:55 - 1:20: Transparent Team Formation

1. Open `/teams`
2. Click **Generate Teams**
3. Show team cards with:
   - Members
   - Rationale
   - Risk flags
   - Score summary
4. Open a team detail page
5. Highlight charter, role rotation, kickoff checklist

## 1:20 - 1:50: AI Team Support Tools

1. On team detail page, use AI widgets:
   - Generate charter
   - Summarize meeting notes into action items
   - Rewrite a message in professional tone
2. Mention mock fallback keeps demo reliable without credentials

## 1:50 - 2:00: Wrap

"Collabor-AI-tor keeps instructors in control, makes team formation transparent, and gives lightweight support tools that help student teams execute better from kickoff through delivery."
